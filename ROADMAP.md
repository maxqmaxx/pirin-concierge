# Product and evidence roadmap

This roadmap describes intended work, not completed functionality or a funding commitment. Release gates are evidence-based; dates will be added only after resources and pilot agreements are confirmed.

## P0 — Safe public baseline

- establish a fresh repository without private grant, survey, partner, or product history;
- publish only synthetic catalogue records and redistributable assets;
- document Apache-2.0/CC BY 4.0 licensing and the proprietary Calivina boundary;
- add reproducible local commands and automated quality/security checks;
- configure a private security-reporting route before public release.

**Exit gate:** allowlist review, secret/PII/licence scan, and repository settings review completed with dated evidence.

## P1 — `v0.1.0-trl4` laboratory demonstrator

- integrate REST/OpenAPI, Schema.org JSON-LD, four MCP tools, catalogue-grounded concierge behaviour, and a synthetic booking-request outbox;
- verify that payments, automatic confirmation, and package sales are absent;
- test all synthetic records and public contracts;
- publish a clean-clone procedure, prerelease, short video, transcript, and evidence matrix.

**Exit gate:** every mandatory item in [`docs/trl4-evidence.md`](docs/trl4-evidence.md) is observed on the release commit. Until then, the TRL 4 evidence claim remains pending.

## P2 — TRL 5 controlled regional validation

- during months 1–2, onboard approximately 10 anchor hosts and establish the controlled validation baseline;
- during months 3–5, reach at least 15 live hosts and at least 70 cumulative session attendances;
- obtain informed participation and content rights from the host cohort;
- complete a data-protection assessment and separate pilot environment;
- validate Bulgarian and English journeys with representative devices, connectivity, and data;
- measure reliability, task completion, usability, catalogue quality, and request delivery;
- publish an appropriately anonymised validation report.

**Exit gate:** predefined TRL 5 acceptance criteria are met or deviations are transparently reported.

## P3 — TRL 6 relevant-environment micro-pilot

- during months 6–12, reach at least 25 hosts across 3–5 settlements, with at least 50% women-led hosts;
- operate an end-to-end prototype across multiple Pirin localities for a defined pilot period;
- train hosts and a community activator;
- monitor technical reliability, safety, adoption, accessibility, and operational workload;
- demonstrate interoperability through MCP and OpenAPI;
- preserve host control of confirmation and direct payment outside the platform;
- publish the TRL 6 Technical Solution Report and an anonymised evidence package tied to `v0.6.0-trl6`.

**Exit gate:** the technology is demonstrated under representative conditions and the evidence package meets the criteria in [`docs/trl4-to-trl6-roadmap.md`](docs/trl4-to-trl6-roadmap.md).

## Beyond TRL 6

Production hardening, legal/commercial operations, scaling, service-level objectives, inventory guarantees, and continued real-world operation would require separate product and governance decisions. They are not implied by this roadmap.
